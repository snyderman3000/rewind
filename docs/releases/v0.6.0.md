**Every new movie you can watch at home, as soon as you can rent it.**

### Fixed
- **New rentals were missing for days.** Rewind found movies by their US *digital* release date on TMDB. Volunteers enter that date, and it often appears days after a movie reaches rental, so new releases like the ones from this week didn't show up until it did. Rewind now also lists films from US theaters, and films that premiered anywhere, in the last 120 days as soon as JustWatch shows somewhere to stream, rent or buy them. They go to the top of **NEWEST** the day they appear.
- A film without a digital date yet shows its theatrical date as **RELEASED** instead of **DIGITAL**.

### Changed
- The guide holds **250** movies by default (was 120). If your `App/Rewind/data/config.json` still says `"max_movies": 120`, raise it to 250 or more (up to 400). The first load after updating takes a little longer.
