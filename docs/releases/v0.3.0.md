First release of **Rewind**: what's new on streaming, on your Miyoo Mini Plus, styled like the front panel of a VCR.

![Rewind screenshots](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/snyderman3000/rewind/main/docs/screens.png)

### What it does
- **New on streaming:** movies that came out digitally in the last 120 days and are on a subscription service in your country now (Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Max, Hulu and the rest)
- **Scores:** Rotten Tomatoes Tomatometer, IMDb, Metascore and TMDB, on seven-segment readouts
- **The details:** age rating, runtime, genres, director, cast, synopsis and which services carry it
- **Sort** by Tomatometer, newest, popular, IMDb, Metascore, title or runtime. **Filter** by genre, service, minimum score, age rating (up to PG, up to PG-13…), runtime and what's already in your library
- **Trailers:** a VCR-style reel of each movie's stills, plus a QR code that opens the real trailer on your phone
- **Radarr (optional):** press **X** (● REC) on any movie to add it to your Radarr. Rewind shows what you already have and download progress
- Opens instantly from the SD card and refreshes in the background

### Install
1. Download `Rewind-v0.3.0-OnionOS.zip` below.
2. Extract it and copy the `App` folder to the root of your SD card, merging it with the existing `App` folder.
3. Get a free [TMDB API key](https://www.themoviedb.org/settings/api) and a free [OMDb key](https://www.omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx) (for Rotten Tomatoes scores). Copy `App/Rewind/config.example.json` to `App/Rewind/data/config.json` and paste them in. If you open Rewind without keys, it shows QR codes for both sign-up pages.
4. Open **Apps → Rewind** with Wi-Fi on. The first load takes about a minute.

To use Radarr, add `radarr_url` and `radarr_api_key` to the same file. See the [README](https://github.com/snyderman3000/rewind#radarr-optional).

Requires OnionOS 4.2 or newer and Wi-Fi (Miyoo Mini Plus or Mini Flip).
