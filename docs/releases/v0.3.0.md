**Rewind 0.3.0** has a new look: the VCR front panel.

![Rewind screenshots](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/snyderman3000/rewind/main/docs/screens.png)

### New look
- Every screen sits inside a silver VCR deck. Across the top: the tape slot and a glowing vacuum-fluorescent display with the clock and PLAY / REC / REW, channel (the movie's number in the list), network and battery indicators. Along the bottom: deck keys.
- Scores are seven-segment readouts, with the faint unlit segments of a real VCR display.
- A proper VHS tape, labeled "BE KIND, REWIND", rewinds while the guide loads and on the blue-screen sign-off. It's also the new app icon.
- Adding to Radarr is now **● REC**, and B is **⏏ EJECT**.
- The trailer reel keeps the VCR on-screen display and tracking noise.

The buttons haven't changed.
