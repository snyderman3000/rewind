**Search any movie, and a guide that keeps up with what's new.**

![Searching in Rewind](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/snyderman3000/rewind/main/docs/search.png)

### New
- **Search:** press **SELECT** on the shelf and type a title on the on-screen keyboard to find any movie, however old. Results show scores, details and which services carry it, and **X** records it to Radarr. Your last five searches are one press away.
- **ARRIVED dates:** Rewind now notices when an older film reaches a streaming service between refreshes. **NEWEST** puts it at the top and the details screen shows the day it arrived.

### Fixed
- **NEWEST got stuck on an old movie.** Rewind kept only the 120 most popular releases of the last 120 days, so this week's releases could be crowded out by established hits, and big films that reach Netflix or Max months after their rental release were never listed. The guide now fills from three windows: the last two weeks, the last 120 days and the past year.
- `rewind.log` now records what each refresh found (and any refresh error), so problems are easier to track down.

### Changed
- **SELECT** is now search. To refresh the guide by hand, press **START** and choose **REFRESH GUIDE** at the bottom of the menu. It still refreshes by itself every few hours.

The first refresh after updating rebuilds the guide; ARRIVED dates start from that point on.
