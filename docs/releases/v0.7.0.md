**NEWEST now means new.**

### Fixed
- **Films whose digital release is still to come were listed first.** A scheduled digital date (like The Odyssey's in November) sorted ahead of everything, and a pre-order made the film look available. Dates that haven't happened yet are ignored now, and a film isn't listed until it can be rented or streamed.
- **Last year's films, and catalog films like Avengers: Endgame, showed up as new.** 0.6 counted the first time Rewind saw a film as the day it arrived, so raising `max_movies` stamped dozens of older films "today". It also took TMDB's 4K re-release and anniversary-showing dates at face value. Rewind now dates every film by its **first** US home release and lists only films whose first home release is in the last 120 days. Films first released more than two years ago are never listed.
- Films with no digital date on TMDB yet (it often lags by days) still show up as soon as they can be rented or streamed, dated by the day Rewind first saw them that way. The details screen shows **IN THEATERS** and **AT HOME BY**.

### Changed
- Older films that only now reach Netflix or Max are no longer listed. Use **SELECT** to search for those.
- The ARRIVED date is gone.
- The first launch after updating re-reads every film's details, so it takes a few minutes. After that, refreshes are quick again.
