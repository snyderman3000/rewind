**Rentals are on the shelf now too.**

### New
- **Rent and buy releases:** Rewind used to list only movies on a subscription service. Now every new digital release is on the shelf, including ones you can only rent or buy so far. Rentals are marked **$**, and their details show where to rent or buy them (Apple TV, Amazon, Google Play…).
- **WATCH ON filter** (START menu): streaming + rental, streaming only, or rent/buy only.
- The **service** filter (L2/R2) includes rental stores.
- When a rental moves to a subscription service, **NEWEST** puts it back on top with an **ARRIVED** date.

The guide still holds 120 movies by default. Now that rentals are included, you may want more: set `"max_movies"` in `App/Rewind/data/config.json` (up to 400; more means a longer first load).
