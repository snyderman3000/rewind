First release of **Rewind**, a new-on-streaming movie guide for OnionOS on the Miyoo Mini Plus.

![Rewind screenshots](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/snyderman3000/rewind/main/docs/screens.png)

### Install
1. Download `Rewind-v0.1.0-OnionOS.zip` below.
2. Extract it and copy the `App` folder to the root of your SD card, merging it with the existing `App` folder.
3. Get a free [TMDB API key](https://www.themoviedb.org/settings/api) and a free [OMDb key](https://www.omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx) (for Rotten Tomatoes scores), and put them in `App/Rewind/data/config.json` (copy `config.example.json`).
4. Open **Apps → Rewind** with Wi-Fi on.

### Highlights
- Recent digital releases that are on a subscription streaming service in your region now
- Rotten Tomatoes Tomatometer, IMDb, Metascore, age rating, runtime, cast, director and services
- Sort by score, date, popularity, title or runtime; filter by genre, service, minimum Tomatometer, age rating and runtime
- A VCR-style trailer reel of stills, plus a QR code that opens the real trailer on your phone
- Cached on the SD card: opens instantly and refreshes in the background
